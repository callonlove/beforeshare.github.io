Privacy first

Your content stays on your iPhone.

BeforeShare processes screenshots, photos, PDFs, prompts, and detection results locally. File sources and exports may use protected temporary storage, and recognition rules you choose to save stay on your device. Optional model downloads send network/request information, including your IP address, to the download service. See our privacy policy for storage and deletion details.
