Common Questions

Does BeforeShare upload my content?

No. BeforeShare is designed for on-device processing. Screenshots, photos, PDFs, prompts, OCR results, and placeholder mappings are not uploaded by BeforeShare.

How do I manage or cancel a subscription?

Open the Settings app on your iPhone, tap your Apple ID, choose Subscriptions, then select BeforeShare. Apple manages subscription billing and cancellation.

How do I request a refund?

Refunds are handled by Apple. Visit reportaproblem.apple.com, sign in with your Apple ID, and choose the BeforeShare purchase.

Why does detection miss something?

Automatic detection can vary based on image quality, language, formatting, lighting, and document layout. Always review detected areas before exporting a file.

Can I restore a previous purchase?

Yes. Use the Restore Purchases option in the app. Make sure you are signed in with the Apple ID used for the original purchase.

Troubleshooting

  • Update to the latest version of BeforeShare from the App Store.
  • Update iOS if your device has a pending system update.
  • Restart the app if import, export, or purchase status appears stuck.
  • For photo or file access issues, check iOS Settings for BeforeShare permissions.
  • For subscription issues, confirm your Apple ID and App Store payment status.

Safety Note

BeforeShare helps reduce accidental disclosure, but you are responsible for reviewing the final file or prompt before sharing it. For highly sensitive legal, medical, financial, or workplace material, check the exported result carefully.
