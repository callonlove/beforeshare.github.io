Privacy Policy

BeforeShare is designed to clean private information before sharing without collecting your personal data.

Our Privacy Commitment

BeforeShare does not collect, store, or transmit your personal data.

OCR, face detection, text redaction, PDF flattening, prompt anonymization, and metadata stripping happen on your device. Your screenshots, photos, PDFs, and text are not uploaded by BeforeShare.

Data We Do Not Collect

  • Name, email address, or phone number
  • Photos, screenshots, PDFs, or exported files
  • AI prompts, anonymized text, or placeholder mappings
  • GPS location or photo metadata
  • Usage analytics or behavioral data
  • Advertising identifiers or cross-app tracking data

What Stays On Your Device

Data Storage Deleted When
Imported screenshots, photos, and PDFs Device memory during processing You close the workflow or export
OCR and detection results Device memory during review You close the workflow
AI Prompt placeholder mappings Device memory during the session The app goes to the background or session ends
Redacted export files Only where you choose to save or share them You control deletion

Services We Use

BeforeShare uses Apple StoreKit 2 for purchases and subscription status. Apple handles payment information and App Store account data. BeforeShare does not receive your full payment details.

The app does not include third-party analytics SDKs, advertising SDKs, or tracking SDKs.

Children's Privacy

BeforeShare is rated 4+ and does not knowingly collect personal data from children.

Changes To This Policy

If our practices change, we will update this policy and post the new version on this page.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact support@beforeshare.app.
