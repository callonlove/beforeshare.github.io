Privacy Policy

BeforeShare is designed to clean private information before sharing without collecting your personal data.

Our Privacy Commitment

BeforeShare does not collect, store, or transmit your personal data.

All processing — OCR, face detection, text redaction, PDF flattening, and metadata stripping — happens entirely on your device. Your screenshots, photos, PDFs, and text never leave your iPhone.

Data We Do NOT Collect

  • ❌ We do not collect your name, email, or phone number
  • ❌ We do not collect your photos, screenshots, or PDFs
  • ❌ We do not collect your AI prompts or anonymized text
  • ❌ We do not collect your location (GPS)
  • ❌ We do not collect usage analytics or behavioral data
  • ❌ We do not use third-party analytics SDKs
  • ❌ We do not use advertising SDKs
  • ❌ We do not track you across other apps or websites

What Stays On Your Device

Data Storage Deleted When
Screenshots/photos/PDFs you import Memory only (RAM) When you close the screen or export
OCR results and detection data Memory only When you close the screen
AI Prompt mapping table Memory only When the app goes to background
Redacted export files Only if you save to Photos You control deletion

App Store Privacy Disclosure

BeforeShare is classified as "Data Not Collected" on the App Store.

On-Device Processing & Third-Party AI Services

BeforeShare does not send any of your data to any third-party AI service.

  • All detection and anonymization — including the AI Prompt Cleaner, OCR, and personal-information detection — runs entirely on your device, using on-device rules and Apple's built-in frameworks.
  • No text, photos, screenshots, PDFs, OCR output, detected personal information, or anonymized content is ever uploaded to, or sent to, any server or any AI service (including but not limited to ChatGPT, OpenAI, Claude, Anthropic, Gemini, or Google AI services). BeforeShare does not call, integrate, or communicate with any such service.
  • When you use the AI Prompt Cleaner, your text is analyzed on this device only. You then copy the cleaned text yourself and paste it into whichever app you choose. BeforeShare does not transmit anything on your behalf.
  • The app contains no third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs, and makes no network requests that carry your personal data.

Because nothing leaves your device, no permission is required and no data is shared with third parties.
