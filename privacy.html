Privacy Policy

BeforeShare processes your private content on your device. This policy explains local storage, optional model downloads, purchases, and support.

Our Privacy Commitment

BeforeShare does not upload the private content you process. You may choose to save names and aliases in a local recognition library.

All processing — OCR, face detection, text redaction, PDF flattening, and metadata stripping — happens entirely on your device. BeforeShare does not upload your screenshots, photos, PDFs, or text. Copies you choose to save, share, or paste into another app are managed by that destination.

Content the App Does Not Upload

  • ❌ The app does not upload names, email addresses, or phone numbers found in your content
  • ❌ The app does not upload your photos, screenshots, or PDFs
  • ❌ The app does not upload your AI prompts or anonymized text
  • ❌ The app does not upload location metadata from your files
  • ❌ The app does not send usage analytics or behavioral data
  • ❌ We do not use third-party analytics SDKs
  • ❌ We do not use advertising SDKs
  • ❌ We do not track you across other apps or websites

App Store Privacy Disclosure

On-device processing does not upload your private content. Optional model downloads send network and request information to the download service, as described below. Local content processing and download-service requests are separate practices. See Apple's privacy definitions for how App Store privacy details describe data handling.

On-Device Processing & Third-Party AI Services

BeforeShare does not send the private content you process to a third-party AI service.

  • All detection and anonymization — including the AI Prompt Cleaner, OCR, and personal-information detection — runs entirely on your device, using on-device rules and Apple's built-in frameworks. Optional additional detection uses the Privacy Filter model with ONNX Runtime on this device.
  • No text, photos, screenshots, PDFs, OCR output, detected personal information, or anonymized content is ever uploaded to, or sent to, any server or any AI service (including but not limited to ChatGPT, OpenAI, Claude, Anthropic, Gemini, or Google AI services). BeforeShare does not call remote AI inference services for your content.
  • When you use the AI Prompt Cleaner, your text is analyzed on this device only. You then copy the cleaned text yourself and paste it into whichever app you choose. BeforeShare does not transmit anything on your behalf.
  • The app contains no third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs. Optional model downloads send network and request information to the download service; they do not include the content being processed.

Preparing Privacy Filter downloads fixed public model files over HTTPS from Hugging Face and its delivery infrastructure. These requests do not include your text, images, PDFs, OCR results, detected details, or recognition-library entries. After preparation, inference runs locally. You do not need a Hugging Face account to request these files.

The download service receives information such as your IP address and request details. Hugging Face's privacy policy describes its handling of service-use, IP, and device information. Local inference does not mean that no network metadata leaves your device. You can remove the downloaded model in the app's settings.

Subscriptions

If you purchase a Pro subscription or Lifetime access:

  • Payment is charged to your Apple ID via the App Store
  • Subscription management is handled entirely by Apple
  • We do not have access to your payment information

Contacting Support

If you email support, we receive your email address, message, and any details you include so we can respond to your request. Your email provider and ours handle delivery. Do not send private documents, screenshots, credentials, or other sensitive content unless you have already redacted it.

Children's Privacy

BeforeShare is rated 4+ and is not directed specifically at children. Optional model downloads use Hugging Face, whose privacy policy directs users under 13 not to use its services. The content rating does not replace the download provider's requirements.
